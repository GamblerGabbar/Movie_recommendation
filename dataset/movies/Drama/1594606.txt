The Hostage's Hero

Ketika Laut Memanggil. Mereka Harus Memilih

Ship hijackings in the Malacca Strait are on the rise and attracting global attention. Lieutenant Colonel Taufiq is reassigned to Indonesia to lead a maritime patrol aboard the KRI Karel Satsuitubun. While on their mission, they face the hijacking of the MT Pematang tanker by a group of pirates. Amidst the dangerous task, the soldiers are faced with a dilemma between national responsibility and family.

Director: Revo S. Rurut

Cast: Donny Alamsyah Rifky Balweel Aditya Herpavi Rachman
