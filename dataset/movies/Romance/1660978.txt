Se upp!

En romcom om syn och vinklar

When Carl realizes he has become invisible, a struggle begins over time and space. With the help of Stella, the only one who can see him, he must solve the mystery of how he became invisible and how to become visible again. Lund becomes the arena for strangeness, love and bells.

Director: David Sjödin

Cast: Julia Kjellgren Sten Hjalmar Lundblad Karlsson Pontus Weibull Moberger
