Alpha and Omega 2: A Howl-iday Adventure

Kate &amp; Humphrey and their three wolf cubs (Smokey, Claudette and Runt) are happily preparing to celebrate their first winter holidays together when their smallest cub, Runt, mysteriously disappears. They must now go on a new journey across the wilderness to find Runt before the winter festivities begin at home.

Director: Richard Rich

Cast: Ben Diskin Marieve Herington Liza West
