Catch the Wind

Fairytale is a sweeping romantic story.

A travelling Irish comedian (O’Donoghue) and a barmaid (Raison) find each other in the urban sprawl of New York City. When fate intervenes, they're confronted with a decision which will determine their destiny.

Director: John Eyres

Cast: Anna Popplewell Archie Panjabi Colin O'Donoghue
