Singh vs Kaur 2

Singh vs Kaur 2 is an upcoming Indian Punjabi-language romantic comedy film directed by Navaniat Singh and produced by Gippy Grewal's Humble Motion Pictures and SVF Entertainment. Serving as a standalone sequel to the 2013 film Singh vs Kaur, it stars Gippy Grewal and Shehnaaz Gill, scheduled for worldwide release on 24th April 2026.
