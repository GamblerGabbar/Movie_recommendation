USER

In 2030, you can be anybody, anybody can be you. In 2035, your body is no longer your own.

In the not too distant future, a man without hope attempts to save the life he was hired to end with HELPER, a world changing technology that allows anyone to possess another's body at the host's request.

Director: Isaac Sparks

Cast: Marlowe Chan-Reeves Sinead MacInnes Simon Bundock
