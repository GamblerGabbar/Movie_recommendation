The Movie Girl

Don’t blink. She’s stepping off the screen.

After fleeing her villainous husband on-screen, a silent film star manifests in the real world. Now a fish out of water in the 21st century, she must navigate a world of sound and color to find the love she was always promised.

Director: Yeva Khadishyan

Cast: Julia Harutyunyan Harutyun Hovhannisyan Hrant Shirinyan
