Cinco Tipos de Medo

Five lives united by fear.

Murilo, a grieving musician, falls for Marlene, a nurse trapped in an abusive relationship with criminal Sapinho. Their fates intertwine with Luciana, a vengeful cop, and Ivan, a lawyer with hidden motives, as five lives collide on an irreversible path.

Director: Bruno Bini

Cast: Bárbara Colen Rui Ricardo Diaz Bella Campos
