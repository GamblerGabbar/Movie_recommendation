Ji Wu

It tells the story of Fang Cheng accompanying her fiancé Su Xing to a strange old house to meet her future in-laws, but found that Su's mother, who only appeared in the middle of the night, had died many years ago and her funeral had never been held. And Su's father was obviously alive, but he insisted on holding a death sacrifice for himself...

Director: Chen Ming

Cast: Pang Zhenqi Jingjing Zhang Zhang Yan Hua
