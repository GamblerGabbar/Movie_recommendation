The Bridge

Foued, a young director, helps his friend Tita by filming his hip-hop video. During a shooting scene with Safa, they find a packet of cocaine. They decide to sell it in small quantities at a trendy nightclub in Tunis. Every night, they cross the bridge. But things take an unexpected turn.

Director: Walid Mattar

Cast: Seif Omrane Mohamed Amine Hamzaoui Sarra Hannachi
