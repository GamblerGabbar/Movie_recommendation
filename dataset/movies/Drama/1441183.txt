North Station

A lonely oceanarium worker, 29-year-old Sveta is afraid of close relationships, replaces them with casual one-night stands and lives on autopilot. One day, she is asked to come forward with accusations of harassment against a university professor, Pavel Rudin, with whom the girl once had an affair. But it turns out that Sveta is still in love with Rudin and is ready to defend him at all costs. But to do this, she will have to face her past...

Director: Sergey Ovechko

Cast: Daria Alypova Sergey Volkov Valeriya Repina
