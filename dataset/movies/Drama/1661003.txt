Feeling

After losing his son in the Itaewon disaster, taxi driver Seongjun endures a numb, hollow routine, estranged from his wife as well. Then, an unexpected connection with Min-ah, an aspiring actress, begins to gently unsettle his tightly closed heart, and ultimately breathes life back into the part of him that had stopped living.

Director: Jung Hyung-suk

Cast: Park Kee-ryung Kim Tae-hee Yoon Bok-in
