Metropolitan Ride

Soobin finally lands a job! With a fluttering heart, she steps into the elevator on her first day. However, countless obstacles still lie between her and her destination: the 7,891st floor. This handmade sci-fi film, created with quiet persistence using 100% analog methods, was made over the past two years, coinciding with the rise of AI as it began to threaten art.

Director: Kim Jun-yong

Cast: Jung Yi-ju 황정화
