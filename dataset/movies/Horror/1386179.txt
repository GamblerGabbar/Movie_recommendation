Songko

A Drop Dead Gorgeous Tale From North Sulawesi.

Mikha and her entire family were forced to be expelled from the village because of accusations by the villagers about Helsye, her stepmother, who was thought to be able to transform into Songko. Mikha also tries to clear her family's good name, including Helsye, who she loves very much.

Director: Gerald Mamahit

Cast: Annette Edoarda Imelda Therinne Fergie Brittany
