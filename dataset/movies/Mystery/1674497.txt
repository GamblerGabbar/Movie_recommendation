Fingered!

Sooner or later, every criminal gets fingered.

Disgruntled detective Janet Wolfe must interrogate her own wife Hannah Foxx over the murders of Janet's ex-wives. Things heat up as the couple's baggage begins to unravel while Janet attempts to get to the bottom of the case, even if that means destroying her marriage.

Director: Isa Gregory

Cast: Eliazah-Imari Dixon Mia Bruno Keira Mitchell
