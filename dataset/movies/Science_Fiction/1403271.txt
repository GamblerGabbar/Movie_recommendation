Serena

Desperate to launch her company into the future with resounding success, a CEO commissions tech engineer Holly  to build her an AI model. However, before being implemented it must be tested. Down-on-his-luck and funds, former rock star, Chris Sadowsky, is asked to be one of the testers — with devastating repercussions.

Director: Rob Alicea

Cast: Andi Matichak Steven Strait Ashleigh Murray
