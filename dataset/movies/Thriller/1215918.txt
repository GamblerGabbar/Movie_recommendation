Blackwater Lane

There is a place where the supernatural is real.

An American school teacher in England drives past an abandoned car late one night, unaware that the driver was the victim of a murder. As she comes to terms with the realization, seemingly supernatural events start to affect the gothic manor she lives in with her husband.

Director: Jeff Celentano

Cast: Minka Kelly Dermot Mulroney Maggie Grace
