Picture Perfect Sister-in-Law

Three years after escaping a notorious child abductor known as the 'Carousel Kiler', former investigative journalist Parker Bennington has rebuilt her life as a university lecturer near her brother's family. But when disturbing symbols from the past—carousel music boxes—start appearing, and her sister-in-law begins acting strangely, Parker suspects someone is targeting her again.

Director: Dave Thomas

Cast: Eddie Davenport Analisa Wall Nick Clark
