Descend Beyond

To understand the human condition, one must rise above it!

Talbot Grimes, a young man filled with unconstrained ambitions, secures himself a position within the British East India Company. He discovers another realm spilling into their own, which sparks an unconventional fascination in him.

Cast: Noah Christensen Mikkel Vang-Busch Simon Kofod
