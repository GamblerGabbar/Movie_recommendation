The Bell: Panggilan Untuk Mati

When a stolen bell unleashes the vengeful ghost of Panebok in Belitong, a shaman’s last descendant must recover the artifact and master forbidden rituals to stop its bloody rampage—before nine lives are claimed, including his own.

Director: Jay Sukmo

Cast: Safira Ratu Sofya Bhisma Mulia Mathias Muchus
