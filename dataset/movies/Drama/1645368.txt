The Abandonment

Everyone knows the name of Samuel Paty, but few people really know his story. On October 16, 2020, Samuel Paty, professor of History-Geography, was murdered at the end of his college. In the light of investigations and trials, this film looks back on his last eleven days, and the gear that led to his tragic death.

Director: Vincent Garenq

Cast: Antoine Reinartz Emmanuelle Bercot Nedjim Bouizzoul
