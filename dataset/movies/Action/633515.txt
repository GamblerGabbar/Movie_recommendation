The Forgotten Battle

In the final years of World War II, the lives of a traitor, a pilot, and a resistance fighter become intertwined as they fight to survive.

During WWII's crucial Battle of the Scheldt, the lives of a glider pilot, a Nazi soldier and a reluctant Resistance recruit tragically intersect.

Director: Matthijs van Heijningen Jr.

Cast: Gijs Blom Jamie Flatters Susan Radder
