Banana: The Whole Bunchy Affair

THE 1ST FILM BY RYAN GROSS. RIPE, UNPEELED, AND SHOWN IN BUNCHES

Ryan Gross' complete cut combining Banana, Bananas, and Banana 3: The Final Stem follows Ben, a man tormented by an incident from his past involving a banana. Will Ben reconcile his past trauma with bananas and learn to coexist with them, or will he be overcome by revenge, and slip into madness?

Director: Ryan Gross

Cast: Josh McCleaf Angel Ortiz Sunny Almeyda
