Kolaiseval

A village located in the Thiruvannamalai district has followed a belief for over 200 years. If a woman in the village becomes pregnant, it is customary to cook Pongal and sacrifice a goat to their family deity, which is located in the middle of a large forest nearby.  Now the heroine of the story is nine months pregnant. As per the tradition, the family travels to the temple. However, the forest area surrounding the temple is known for many anti-social activities.  What happens to the family that goes to this mysterious place? Why is the temple located deep inside the forest?

Director: VR. Thudhivaanan

Cast: Kalaiyarasan Deepa Balu Balasaravanan
