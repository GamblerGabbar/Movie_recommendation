Bluebird

One night, while working as a handyman at a bizarre medical facility, a lonely man named Charles meets a young woman named Chloe. The two share an immediate connection as she recounts having met him in a dream. When Chloe disappears, Charles sets out to find her in a strange world that seems intent on keeping them apart.

Director: Jay Arden Black

Cast: David McElwee Avery Joy Davis Tony Bertele
