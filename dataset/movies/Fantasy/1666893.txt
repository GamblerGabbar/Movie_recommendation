Candy and the Pizza Ggirl

Candy and the Pizza Ggirl is A Hindi Movie.  The story unfolds over one surreal, chaotic full-moon Saturday night in Mumbai, where a single small mistake or catastrophic incident triggers a chain reaction of interconnected, absurd, and unpredictable events. Multiple eccentric characters' lives collide in a fragmented, neon-lit world of illusion, bad decisions, excess, and blurring realities. No one is truly in control; everyone lives in their own version of reality, and chaos spirals as parallel paths overlap.

Director: Akhil Kapur

Cast: Priya Banerjee Ninad Kamat Dara Sandhu
