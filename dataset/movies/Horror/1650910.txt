Hell Trotter

“Heo Năm Móng” is inspired by a mystical supernatural legend from the Khmer community in Vietnam’s Mekong Delta, conveying themes of karma and consequence, and warning of the unpredictable repercussions that arise from misplacing one’s faith and beliefs.

Director: Lưu Thành Luân

Cast: Võ Tấn Phát Trần Ngọc Vàng Huỳnh Thanh Thuỷ
