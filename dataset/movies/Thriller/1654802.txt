Return from Tomorrow

Randy Moore’s follow-up to the (in)famously filmed-in-secret Disney World fever-dream Escape from Tomorrow is an absurdist paranoid conspiracy thriller featuring the original cast in a completely new scenario. After the loss of his father, a middle-aged dad spirals into a Lynchian crisis of masculinity while on family vacation in Miami. As his inadequacies and unspoken desires drift him further away from his wife, he uncovers a mysterious plot involving his father, the gas company, cocaine, prostitution, and . . . Walt?

Director: Randy Moore

Cast: Roy Abramsohn Elena Schuber Annet Mahendru
