Terror Firma

Let madness blossom.

During a lockdown, Lola, a struggling artist, is forced to wait it out with her brother Louis and his crazy roommate Cage. After a mysterious batch of seeds arrive threatening to change the course of existence, Lola must fight to survive a surreal landscape of external and internal forces.

Director: Jake Macpherson

Cast: Faye Nightingale Max Carpenter Robert Brettenaugh
