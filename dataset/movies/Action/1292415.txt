Beast

Legends are made in the cage.

MMA legend Patton James, now a commercial fisherman, is pulled back into the cage when his brother is in danger. Reuniting with his old coach Sammy, he commits to one final fight in one championship against it's brutal champion Xavier Grau.

Director: Tyler Atkins

Cast: Daniel MacPherson Luke Hemsworth Russell Crowe
