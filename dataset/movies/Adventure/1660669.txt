My own worst enemy

You can't go back and change the beginning but you can start where you are and change the ending.

In 90s working-class Bromley, 15 year old Danny bullies classmates to hide his own pain. Behind his tough exterior lies a fractured family and dreams of escape. To get away from this life he goes skateboarding and exploring with his friends around south London.

Director: Chungus steak filler the 3rd

Cast: Oscar de Cruz Soul Ubergs Zach Hobbs-McHugh
