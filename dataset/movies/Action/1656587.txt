TJPW Spring Tour 2026 ~Next Door~ in Fukuoka

TJPW Spring Tour 2026 ~Next Door~ in Fukuoka was a professional wrestling event promoted by Tokyo Joshi Pro-Wrestling (TJPW). It was held on April 5, 2026 at ACROS Fukuoka in Fukuoka, Japan.

Cast: Miyu Yamashita Shoko Nakajima Yuki Arai
