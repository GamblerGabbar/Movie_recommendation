Ayah, Ini Arahnya Ke Mana, Ya?

A daughter grows up in a house with a father who is always there but never truly present. When a family crisis forces her to bear the burden of adulthood, she must learn to live without the direction that should come from the figure she misses most, her own father.

Director: Kuntz Agus

Cast: Mawar Eva De Jongh Rey Bong Dwi Sasono
