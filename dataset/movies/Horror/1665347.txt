Bloodsuckers

Being a vampire slayer is a full time job

Bliss is 26, lonely, and convinced she’s a vampire slayer. But when her housemate Molly takes her relentless bullying a step too far, Bliss comes to the tenuous conclusion that perhaps the demons she’s been hunting have been under her roof the whole time…

Director: Rosie Brear

Cast: Thaddea Graham Laura Marcus Amber Grappy
