Mirror

He ran from himself, and from love; only his son holds the way back.

An aging and formerly successful bodybuilder, struggles with failing health while refusing to accept that his glory days are behind him. When his son unexpectedly enters his life, he is challenged to find meaning in life outside of bodybuilding.

Director: Todor Chapkanov

Cast: Magnus Samuelsson Matias Varela Albert Rosin Harson
