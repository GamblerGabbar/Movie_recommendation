Tired of Being a Nerd

Airton used to be the favorite target of jokes and teasing from his classmates during his school years. Years later, when the time comes for the big class reunion party, he is declared the guest of honor. Suddenly revered by his classmates, little does he know the real intentions behind the celebration.

Director: Gualter Pupo

Cast: Fernando Caruso Bia Guedes Cissa Guimarães
