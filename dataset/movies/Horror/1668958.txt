Rosario

When Beth and Ave's mother died, a relative unknowingly put a rosary in her mother’s hand. According to superstitious belief, the rosary needs to be cut before burying the corpse, but they forgot to do so. Now, the curse of death follows Beth's family one by one. Desperate to end the curse, Beth must uncover the truth and find a way to break it before death consumes them too.

Director: Roni S. Benaid

Cast: Aubrey Caraan Jairus Aquino Lance Carr
