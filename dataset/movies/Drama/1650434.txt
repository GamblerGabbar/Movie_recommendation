Sales log

Do-eun, a door-to-door saleswoman in her thirties, visits the home of a former classmate with whom she has little to no rapport, simply to make up for her unsatisfactory sales performance. A few days later, the classmate's mother, Su-jung, comes looking for her.

Director: Kang Min-a

Cast: Na Ae-jin Min Hyo-gyeong Shin Ji-i
