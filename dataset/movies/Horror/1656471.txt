The Night Mother Returns Home

Srey Neang is a service worker struggling to care for her 7-year-old daughter, Srey Roth, often returning home late at night. One evening, she collapses and dies inside their home, unnoticed by her daughter. Believing her mother is only sick, Srey Roth sleeps beside her for three days. Srey Neang’s spirit remains behind, protecting her child and haunting those whose cruelty tore them apart, refusing to leave her daughter alone.

Director: Kou Darachan

Cast: Seanghuy Heab Vann Samphors
