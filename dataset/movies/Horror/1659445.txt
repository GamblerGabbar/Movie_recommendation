The Hunger

When it awakens, someone always pays the price

Victoria, a seamstress exhausted by hardship, sustains the fragile existence of her brother Lucas, who lives confined to a wheelchair in an environment of extreme poverty. However, prolonged deprivation awakens something dark: a monstrous transformation in Lucas that drives him to devour everything.

Director: Mario García

Cast: Mariale Adrianzén Nicolás Bazán Cesar Marticorena
