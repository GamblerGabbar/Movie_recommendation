Reminiscencias

Lucas, a 44-year-old priest, returns to San Juan to replace a deceased colleague. The return to her native province brings with it memories of the relationship he had with Mateo when they were teenagers. Mateo, now a 45-year-old psychologist, finds out that Lucas arrived in the province and decides to go see him.

Director: Agustín Miranda

Cast: Rodrigo Gastón Salas Martín Mambretti Tomas Linares
