Kyojo Reunion

Kyojo is a school that trains future police officers. In its closed room, where unqualified students are weeded out, students with dreams, hopes, and secrets face off against the strict instructor, Kimichi Kazama, who can see through even the smallest lies. This is the first of a two-part film adaptation of the TV drama series "Kyojo."

Director: Isamu Nakae

Cast: Takuya Kimura Keito Tsuna Kyoko Saito
