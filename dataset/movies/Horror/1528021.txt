Panor 2

The ultimate art of the devil.

After Panor renounces the power of the Three-Eyed God, her life is reset — her memory wiped clean. She begins a new chapter, living her dream of studying at a teacher's college, with Piak quietly watching over and helping her from afar.

Director: Putipong Saisikaew

Cast: Cherprang Areekul Jackrin Kungwankiatichai Pimmada Chaisaksoen
