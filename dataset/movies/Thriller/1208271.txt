Wrangler

Hayoung, a successful dog trainer, is visited by her sister, Sora, who has a criminal record for murder. As Sora's bizarre behavior unsettles the training center and Hayoung's daily routine, buried truths and repressed desires start to rise to the surface.

Director: Seo Eun-sun

Cast: Choi Seung-yoon Kim Seung-hwa Oh Jung-hwan
