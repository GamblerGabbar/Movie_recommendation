Sunshine Women's Choir

As the birthday song plays, a baby girl is born inside a women's prison, welcomed by four inmates, each burdened by a painful past. When a rebellious newcomer arrives, clashes give way to friendship. After the baby is diagnosed with an eye disease, the inmates form a prison choir to gift the baby girl a final memory.

Director: Gavin Lin

Cast: Ivy Chen Judy Ongg Chung Hsin-ling
