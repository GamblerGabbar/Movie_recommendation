Shaka Oh Shaka

A meeting at a concert changes everything. Ocel, a simple college student, never expected to become the lover of her idol, Shaka. Their closeness, which began with admiration, blossomed into a mutually reinforcing love. However, their relationship was tested by the realities of life and the swift currents of time. Will they survive, or will they find their way through separation?

Director: Dinna Jasanti

Cast: Kiesha Alvaro Arla Ailani Adzana Shaliha
